The method

Simple rules,
applied every week.

No secret formula. Forge follows the boring, proven basics of strength programming and does the bookkeeping for you.

01

Split by your schedule

Two or three days get full-body sessions. Four days become upper / lower. Five days run push, pull, legs, then upper and lower.

02

Volume by your clock

Every set, rest and changeover is timed. If a session runs long, Forge trims sets from the bottom of the day first, never the big lifts.

03

Balance across the week

Squat, hinge, lunge, push and pull in both planes, plus core and carries, so no muscle group gets left behind.
