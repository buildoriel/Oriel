      FAQ

      Good questions

      Anything else? Our team answers within one working day via the newsletter form below.

      Will the talks be recorded?

      Yes. Every main-stage and track talk is recorded and shared with ticket holders within two weeks. Workshops are not recorded, so the room can stay candid.

      Can I transfer my ticket to a colleague?

      Absolutely. Name changes are free until 7 May 2027 from the link in your confirmation email. After that, bring photo ID and we’ll sort it at the desk.

      What is the refund policy?

      Full refunds until 1 March 2027, 50% until 1 April 2027. After that tickets are non-refundable but always transferable.

      Do you offer scholarships?

      We reserve 60 free Conference Passes for students, educators and people from groups under-represented in tech and design. Applications are reviewed blind by a volunteer panel.

      Is the venue accessible?

      All halls are step-free with lifts to the terrace. Every talk has live captions, there is a quiet room on the mezzanine, and gender-neutral bathrooms on each floor.

      Do I need to bring a laptop?

      Only for hands-on workshops — each workshop page lists its setup. Power and fast Wi-Fi are available everywhere in the building.

      Is there a code of conduct?

      Yes, and we enforce it. Meridian is a harassment-free event for everyone. Staff in coral lanyards are on hand throughout both days.
