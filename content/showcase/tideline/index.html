03 · Why now

Climate regulation is flowing downstream — straight to the factory floor.

RegulationCSRD · CBAM · SB 253

Rules that make large companies account for emissions across their value chain.

Large buyers~40,000

companies in the EU and US now reporting Scope 3 — mostly their suppliers’ emissions.

Their suppliers1.2M

small manufacturers being asked for primary data — with no team, tools or budget to produce it.

  1. 2025First CSRD reports cite supplier data; questionnaires go out
  2. Jan 2026CBAM definitive phase: importers pay for embedded carbon
  3. 2027California SB 253 requires Scope 3 disclosure
  4. 2027+OEMs write emissions data into supplier contracts
The key insight: regulation targets big companies, but the work lands on small ones. Every large buyer that must disclose Scope 3 sends requests to hundreds of suppliers. That turns a compliance niche into a mass market of 1.2 million manufacturers who never planned to buy carbon software.

The moat is step two. Our matching model maps messy line items — “ALU INGOT 6063 12.4T” — to the right factor, and every number carries a source and confidence score, which is why auditors and buyers accept our output. Step three is human-in-the-loop for first reports, then fully self-serve.
