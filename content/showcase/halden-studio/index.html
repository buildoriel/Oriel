02 — Studio

The studio

Halden Studio works from a former print shop in Copenhagen K. We take on six to eight commissions a year and stay with each one from the first site walk to the last snag list — because the details that matter most are usually decided on site.

Materials we return to
5

Lime, brick, oak, larch and linen. We would rather know five materials deeply than fifty from a catalogue.

Principles

I.

Light before form

We draw sun paths before we draw walls. Every room is placed for the hour of the day it will be used most.

II.

Few materials, honestly joined

Surfaces are what they appear to be. Joints are visible, considered and made to be understood.

III.

Built to be repaired

Lime over cement, screws over glue, timber that can be sanded back. Buildings should get better as they age.

IV.

Rooms before objects

We would rather design one generous room well than a house of clever ones. Proportion does most of the work.
